# Business Rules — Resort Booking & Event Management

## Booking Rules
1. Each booking must have a start and end date
2. No double booking for overlapping dates
3. Booking remains "Pending" until 30% of the total is paid
4. 80% refund if cancelled more than 72 hours before start date

## Event Rules
1. Each event must be linked to at least one staff member
2. Events can only be booked between 9:00 AM and 10:00 PM
3. No pets allowed in events
4. Outdoor services depend on weather conditions

## Room Rules
1. Each room has a maximum capacity
2. Room rates vary by weekday/weekend/holiday
3. Rooms are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis

## Car Rental Rules
1. A customer can rent only one car at a time
2. Car type depends on the customer's payment plan
3. Rental charges based on car type, category, and payment method
